# **Project Proposal & Development Recommendation**

## Nepal News Portal (Website + Mobile Apps + Admin CMS)

---

# Introduction
After reviewing all requested features, we found that the project is **much larger than a standard news portal**. It combines multiple products into one platform, including:

* News Portal
* Video Platform
* CMS
* User Management System
* Social Features
* Nepal Utility Services
* Business Directory
* Job Portal
* Health Portal
* News Aggregation Platform
* Landing Page Builder
* Calendar & Reminder System

This is comparable to building platforms like **OnlineKhabar, Setopati, Hamro Patro, Jobs Nepal, and a custom CMS—all in a single project.**

Because of the size of the project, we recommend developing it in **multiple phases** instead of attempting everything in the first release.

This approach significantly reduces the initial investment while allowing the platform to launch earlier and start generating users and revenue.

---

# Why We Recommend Excluding Some Features Initially

Some requested features require a substantial amount of development time and ongoing maintenance. Including them in the first version would increase both the cost and the delivery timeline.

Launching with the core features first provides several advantages:

* Lower initial investment.
* Faster time to market.
* Opportunity to gather user feedback before investing in advanced modules.
* Easier maintenance and testing.
* Reduced project risk.
* Ability to prioritize future features based on actual user demand.

Many successful platforms follow this approach, launching with essential functionality and expanding over time.

# Features Recommended for Phase 2

These features are significantly more complex and are better developed after the platform is live.

## 1. News Aggregation System

Automatically importing news from other websites requires:

* RSS parsing
* API integrations
* Web scraping (where permitted)
* Duplicate detection
* Background processing
* Copyright compliance
* Continuous monitoring

This is essentially a separate product within the platform.

---

## 2. Automatic News Import

The system would periodically fetch news from external sources, categorize it, assign tags, and publish it automatically.

This requires:

* Scheduled background jobs
* Error handling
* AI/manual categorization
* Duplicate checking
* Source management

---

## 3. Dynamic Homepage Builder

Allowing administrators to rearrange homepage sections without developer assistance requires a custom page builder.

This involves:

* Drag-and-drop layouts
* Widget management
* Dynamic rendering
* Layout storage

---

## 4. Dynamic Landing Page Builder

Creating special pages for events such as elections, festivals, or breaking news with custom layouts requires a reusable page-building system.

---

## 5. Stories (Facebook/Instagram Style)

Developing story-style content involves:

* Full-screen media viewer
* Story progression
* Expiration logic
* Media optimization

---

## 6. Live Feed & Live Streaming

Requires integration with streaming platforms or media servers and introduces additional complexity in content delivery and moderation.

---

## 7. Business, Job & Health Directories

These are independent modules with their own:

* Categories
* Search
* Listings
* Approval workflows
* Management interfaces

---

## 8. Polls, Surveys & Quizzes

Require:

* Voting logic
* Result calculations
* User participation tracking
* Reporting

---

## 9. Advanced Analytics

Includes:

* Visitor reports
* Popular content
* User behavior
* Advertisement analytics
* Author performance

# Recommendation for the Nepal Market

Based on our experience, many digital media startups in Nepal prefer to launch with a **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)** due to budget constraints and the need to validate the business before making larger investments.

We recommend:

1. Launch a robust, production-ready news platform with all essential publishing, user, and administrative features.
2. Build an audience, onboard content creators, and generate revenue through advertising.
3. Add advanced capabilities—such as news aggregation, dynamic page builders, stories, live feeds, business directories, and other specialized modules—in later phases based on user demand and business growth.

This phased strategy minimizes upfront costs, accelerates the launch timeline, and allows future investment to be guided by real user engagement rather than assumptions. It also provides a more practical path for startups and media organizations operating within the Nepalese market.
